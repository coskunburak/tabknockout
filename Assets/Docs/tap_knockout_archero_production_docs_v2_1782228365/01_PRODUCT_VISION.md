# Product Vision

## Product Statement

Tap Knockout is a desktop-first 3D arena survivor roguelike where the player survives escalating enemy waves through movement skill, active ability timing, build crafting, and readable combat chaos.

## Player Fantasy

The player fantasy is:

```text
fast arena survival, build crafting, active skill mastery, readable 3D chaos, escalating enemy pressure
```

The player should feel surrounded but not confused. Enemy pressure should climb continuously, while level-up choices and skill cooldown decisions give the player clear ways to change the run.

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Players who enjoy Vampire Survivors-style escalation and upgrade pacing.
- Players who enjoy Soulstone Survivors-style 3D arena combat.
- Players who like short replayable roguelite runs.
- Players who value responsive movement, readable telegraphs, and Hades-like combat clarity.
- Steam players who expect keyboard/mouse controls, performance options, and a clean demo.

Secondary audience:

- Action roguelite players who want more active skill usage than pure auto-combat.
- Players who enjoy build discovery, cooldown planning, and boss milestone pressure.

## MVP Goal

The MVP target is a 10-15 minute desktop survivor run:

- One 3D arena.
- One playable character.
- WASD movement and mouse aim.
- Dash/evade key.
- Several active skill slots.
- At least 5 enemy archetypes.
- At least 1 elite variant.
- At least 1 boss.
- Wave/timer-based spawn director.
- XP pickups and level-up ability choices.
- Run win/loss result screen.
- Stable 60 FPS target on a mid-range PC.

## Product Pillars

### Readable 3D Pressure

Enemy density can be high, but silhouettes, VFX, ground telegraphs, pickups, damage feedback, and boss warnings must remain readable.

### Active Skill Mastery

The game is not only passive auto-combat. Active cooldown skills, dash timing, mouse aim, and positioning should matter.

### Build Crafting

Every level-up choice should push the player toward a visible combat pattern: projectile spread, area denial, burst cooldowns, survival tanking, dash aggression, summons/traps, or pickup economy.

### Short Replayable Runs

Runs should be compact enough for repeated attempts. The first MVP run target is 10 minutes. Later content can extend to 15-20 minute modes.

### Original Identity

Reference games inform pacing and readability only. Content, visuals, naming, skills, UI, and balance must be original.

## Business Direction

The MVP is Steam/demo-friendly. Monetization, ads, mobile stores, daily economies, and IAP are not core MVP requirements. Future commercial direction should prioritize a premium or demo-to-full PC path unless the user explicitly chooses another model.

## Legacy Note

The older mobile room-based concept is deprecated for MVP. Useful systems from that direction, such as abilities, enemies, bosses, damage, health, projectiles, ScriptableObject configs, pooling, QA, and content pipeline planning, should be preserved and migrated into the survivor structure.
